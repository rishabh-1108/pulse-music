// STARK INDUSTRIES

INNOVATION LAB

From a cave with a box of scraps to the most advanced nanotechnology suit ever conceived, the Iron Man suit evolution represents humanity's greatest leap in engineering. Each iteration pushed the boundaries of what was possible.

I
MARK I
2008
CAVE-BUILT PROTOTYPE
Built in captivity by Tony Stark and Yinsen using scrap metal and salvaged weapons parts. Featured flamethrowers, a rudimentary repulsor prototype, and limited flight capability. First demonstrated the Jericho missile system during its escape from the Ten Rings. The spark that ignited a revolution in defense technology.
VII
MARK VII
2012
AUTO-ASSEMBLY SYSTEM
Deployed during the Battle of New York against the Chitauri invasion. Featured laser-guided auto-assembly allowing mid-air suiting at terminal velocity. Equipped with enhanced repulsor output, shoulder-mounted micro-missiles, and advanced targeting HUD. The suit that saved Manhattan.
XLII
MARK XLII
2013
PREHENSILE PROTOCOL
Every piece flies independently and assembles onto Stark's body via magnetic bonding and neural interface. Enabled remote deployment and partial armor activation for the first time. Gold-heavy color scheme distinguished it from predecessors. Allowed Stark to suit up from anywhere within satellite range.
XLIV
MARK XLIV HULKBUSTER
2015
ANTI-HULK MODULAR ARMOR
Modular heavy-combat suit designed by Stark and Banner as a contingency against Hulk rampages. Deployed via orbital satellite drop-ship in Johannesburg. Features reinforced exo-frame, enhanced power core, and seismic impact fists. Modular design allows field repair by swapping damaged components mid-fight.
L
MARK L
2018
NANOTECH FIRST GEN
Breakthrough nanotechnology platform featuring billions of nano-particles stored in a chest-mounted housing unit. Real-time shape-shifting into weapons, shields, and flight surfaces. Deployed during the Battle of Titan against Thanos. Represents the convergence of Stark's nanotech research and Wakandan vibranium science.
LXXXV
MARK LXXXV
2019
FINAL ITERATION
Tony Stark's ultimate masterpiece. Enhanced nanotech with stronger durability, energy-blade generation, and integrated Nano Gauntlet for Infinity Stone handling. Worn during the final Battle of Earth. Designed with the ability to channel the full power of all six Infinity Stones — at the cost of the wielder's life.
RES
RESCUE
2019
PEPPER POTTS' SUIT
Custom-built by Tony Stark for Pepper Potts with a focus on defensive and rescue operations rather than pure combat. Features energy shields, repulsor pulses, and advanced life-saving systems. Blue and silver color scheme differentiates it from the Iron Man line. Deployed during the final battle against Thanos' forces.
LEG
IRON LEGION
2015
DRONE ARMY — AI CONTROLLED
Autonomous drone fleet controlled by the FRIDAY AI system. Designed for mass deployment in large-scale threats and global defense per the Ultron Protocol. Each drone equipped with repulsors, micro-missiles, and surveillance tech. Later upgraded with enhanced AI following the Ultron incident and integrated into the Avengers defense network.

SHURI'S LAB
MOST ADVANCED TECH LAB ON EARTH
Princess Shuri's laboratory is the crown jewel of Wakandan innovation, combining Vibranium science with visionary engineering to produce technology decades ahead of anything else on Earth. Her creations include the Black Panther Habit, vibranium mining devices, maglev weapons, and the synthesis of the Heart-Shaped Herb. The lab served as the primary R&D hub during the Infinity War and Endgame conflicts.

CITY-WIDE POWER INFRASTRUCTURE
Wakanda's energy grid is a Vibranium-powered distributed power network that provides limitless clean energy to every corner of the nation. This same grid powers the cloaking shields that hide Wakanda from the outside world.
