THE BLIP

REVERSED
CASUALTIES: 3.5 BILLION ERASED DURATION: 5 YEARS
Thanos used the completed Infinity Gauntlet to erase half of all living creatures across the entire universe with a single snap, wiping out 3.5 billion beings instantly. The Blip caused the collapse of ecosystems, economies, and governments across the globe. Five years later, the Avengers performed a Time Heist to reverse the snap, restoring all dusted beings — but the temporal paradoxes from this reversal continue to ripple through the multiverse.

2024

SPIDER-MAN SPELL BREACH

CONTAINED
CASUALTIES: 0 DIRECT DIMENSIONS AFFECTED: 3+
Doctor Strange cast a spell at Spider-Man's request to make the world forget his identity. The spell fractured, pulling multiversal variants of Spider-Man's rogues gallery into Earth-199999. The barriers between realities weakened catastrophically before a modified final spell erased all knowledge of Peter Parker from every universe to seal the breach permanently.
Impact: DELTA
3/5
2022

MULTIVERSE OF MADNESS

RESOLVED
CASUALTIES: 12,000+ DARKHOLD USED: YES
The Scarlet Witch, corrupted by the Darkhold, tore through the multiverse chasing America Chavez. Earth-838's entire Illuminati was destroyed and over 12,000 lives were lost across multiple realities. The Darkhold was destroyed across every universe by Doctor Strange, but the dimensional scars remain — Wanda's sacrifice sealed the Darkhold Castle but left lasting fractures in the multiversal fabric.
Impact: OMEGA
5/5
2025

KANG'S TEMPORAL CONQUEST

ONGOING
CASUALTIES: UNCOUNTABLE TIMELINES AT RISK: INFINITE
Kang the Conqueror emerged from the Quantum Realm wielding technology that allows instant traversal of the multiverse. His variants — Immortus, Rama-Tut, and the Council of Kangs — threaten to collapse all branching timelines into a single controlled reality. The TVA has been compromised and the Sacred Timeline is under siege from multiple incursion points simultaneously, with casualties across countless timelines.
Impact: OMEGA
5/5
2026

SECRET WARS

APPROACHING
CASUALTIES: ALL REALITIES THREAT LEVEL: EXISTENTIAL
The convergence of all multiversal crises culminates in Secret Wars — the final incursion where two Earths collide and the Beyonders attempt to end all of reality. Every hero from every universe must unite or face absolute annihilation. The battle for existence itself will determine whether the multiverse survives as one unified reality or shatters into nothingness forever.
