MAJOR BATTLES

HIGH THREAT
BATTLE OF NEW YORK
2012
Manhattan, New York City
Avengers (Assembled)
Loki + Chitauri Army
73 Blocks Evacuated
~1,200 Civilian Casualties
Loki opened a portal above Stark Tower using the Tesseract, allowing a Chitauri army to pour into Manhattan in relentless waves. The Avengers assembled for the first time, fighting back-to-back across the devastated cityscape. Tony Stark lured a nuclear missile through the portal while the team defended the portal device atop Stark Tower. The Chitauri were defeated when Stark destroyed their command ship, severing the alien connection. The battle marked the birth of Earth's mightiest superhero team and changed the world forever.

BATTLE OF SOKOVIA
2015
Sokovia (Floating City)
Avengers + Ultron Defectors
Ultron's Sentinels
Entire City Airlifted
177 Civilian Casualties
Ultron lifted the entire city of Sokovia into the sky, turning it into a meteor weapon aimed at humanity's extinction. Iron Man and the Avengers coordinated civilian evacuation while battling waves of Ultron sentry robots across the floating wreckage. Natasha Romanoff activated repulsor engines built into the city's core to keep it airborne. Vision confronted Ultron's digital consciousness directly while Quicksilver made the ultimate sacrifice protecting civilians. The city was destroyed in a controlled descent, saving all remaining civilians but costing Sokovia its homeland.

BATTLE OF WAKANDA
2018
Wakanda, East Africa
Avengers + Wakandan Army
Thanos + Outrider Army
Vibranium Barrier Deployed
~4,200 Wakandan Casualties
The last line of defense for the Mind Stone, Wakanda faced the full might of Thanos' army as Outriders breached the Vibranium barrier in overwhelming numbers. T'Challa led the combined Wakandan forces alongside Captain America, Black Widow, and Bruce Banner in the Hulkbuster armor. Thor arrived mid-battle wielding Stormbreaker, turning the tide with devastating lightning strikes across the front lines. Despite every effort including Vision's destruction to deny Thanos the Mind Stone, the Mad Titan completed his Infinity Gauntlet. The snap erased half of all life in the universe, leaving the survivors shattered.

BATTLE OF TITAN
2018
Titan (Thanos' Homeworld)
Iron Man, Spider-Man, Strange, Guardians
Thanos (w/ 4 Stones)
Dead Planet, No Civilians
0 Civilian Casualties
On the ruined surface of Thanos' dead homeworld, Iron Man, Spider-Man, Doctor Strange, and the Guardians launched a coordinated ambush against the Mad Titan. The team fought with extraordinary precision, nearly removing the Infinity Gauntlet until Star-Lord's rage broke their coordinated hold. Thanos overwhelmed the exhausted heroes, demonstrating the devastating power of the completed Power, Space, Reality, and Soul Stones. Doctor Strange surrendered the Time Stone in a mysterious bargain, prophesying that it was the only way. The defeat on Titan set the stage for the final reckoning.

BATTLE OF EARTH — ENDGAME
2023
Avengers Compound, Upstate New York
All Avengers + Allied Forces
Thanos + 2014 Army
Time Heist Activated
2 Confirmed Hero Deaths
The most epic battle in human history unfolded when a time-displaced 2014 Thanos destroyed the Avengers Compound and waged war against every hero assembled. The Quantum Realm time heist brought back fallen allies while the original Avengers held the line in desperate defense of the Nano Gauntlet. Captain America wielded Mjolnir as portals opened to reveal restored armies from every corner of the universe. Iron Man made the ultimate sacrifice, stealing the Infinity Stones and snapping Thanos' forces to dust at the cost of his own life. The universe was saved at an immeasurable price.

BATTLE OF XANDAR
2014
Xandar, Nova Empire Capital
Nova Corps + Guardians
Ronan the Accuser + Kree Fleet
Planet-Killer Bomb Deployed
~2 Million Displaced
Ronan the Accuser, empowered by the Power Stone, turned the full might of the Kree Empire against Xandar after the Guardians' failed interception. The Nova Corps fleet was decimated in minutes as Ronan's Dark Aster descended through the atmosphere preparing to obliterate the planet. Star-Lord's audacious dance-off distraction combined with the Guardians sharing the Power Stone's burden destroyed Ronan's hammer. Xandar was saved by the narrowest of margins, but the victory proved temporary when Thanos later devastated the planet to claim the Power Stone.

BATTLE OF KAMAR-TAJ
2022
Kamar-Taj, Nepal
Sorcerers + Doctor Strange
Scarlet Witch (Possessed)
Darkhold Rituals Active
~30 Sorcerer Casualties
Kamar-Taj, the ancient sanctuary of the sorcerers, faced its most devastating assault when the Scarlet Witch corrupted by the Darkhold waged a one-woman siege to capture America Chavez. The defensive wards were shattered as Wanda's dreamwalking power tore through every enchantment the Masters of the Mystic Arts had ever constructed. Hundreds of sorcerers fell defending their home as Wanda tore through the halls with terrifying precision. Doctor Strange confronted alternate versions of himself while Wanda's grief was revealed as the root of her corruption. The Darkhold was ultimately destroyed and Wanda found redemption at the cost of her own life.

BATTLE OF THE QUANTUM REALM
2023
Quantum Realm (Subatomic Dimension)
Ant-Man, Wasp, Avengers
Kang the Conqueror
Quantum Fluctuations Detected
Dimensional Collapse Risk
Deep within the Quantum Realm, Ant-Man and the Wasp discovered a hidden civilization oppressed by Kang the Conqueror, a time-displaced variant with technology far beyond anything on Earth. Kang's ability to manipulate probability and his quantum-powered army made him one of the most dangerous threats the Avengers had ever faced. The battle saw reality itself bending as quantum fluctuations destabilized the entire battlefield. Scott Lang barely escaped total molecular disintegration as the subatomic dimension threatened to collapse. The defeat of Kang sent shockwaves through the multiverse.

BATTLE OF THE CELESTIAL MOON
2021
Titan's Moon, Saturn Orbit
Eternals + Guardians
Abandoned Celestial Tech
Orbital Bombardment
Colony Evacuated
A dormant Celestial technology awakened on Titan's moon, threatening to destabilize Saturn's entire orbital system. The Eternals were called upon alongside the Guardians of the Galaxy to neutralize the rogue cosmic entity. Phastos led the engineering effort to contain the Celestial energy while Ikaris engaged the threat directly in aerial combat across the cratered surface. The battle caused massive tidal disruptions on Titan itself, forcing a complete evacuation of hidden colonies. The Celestial tech was silenced when Sersi channeled the Uni-Mind to transform the threat into inert cosmic dust.

2024
Dark Dimension (Inter-Dimensional)
Doctor Strange + Wong
Dormammu's Lieutenants
Dimensional Barriers Breached
Reality Warping Detected
The barriers between dimensions fractured as Dormammu dispatched his lieutenants to prepare Earth for his return. Doctor Strange and Wong led a coalition of sorcerers into the Dark Dimension itself for the first time in recorded history. The battlefield defied physics as gravity, time, and space shifted without warning under the Dark Dimension's influence. Strange used the Vishanti's remaining power to create safe corridors while Wong held the dimensional gate open against relentless assault. The siege was repelled but the Dark Dimension's tendrils continued to probe Earth's defenses.
