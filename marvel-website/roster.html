IRON MAN
TONY STARK — 1970–2023
The genius billionaire who started it all. From building a suit in a cave to wielding the Infinity Stones, Tony Stark proved that even a man without powers can save the universe. His sacrifice snapped away Thanos and restored half of all life.
IN MEMORY: THE ONE WHO SAVED THE UNIVERSE
BLACK WIDOW
NATASHA ROMANOFF — 1984–2023
From Red Room assassin to Earth's mightiest hero, Natasha Romanoff dedicated her life to protecting others. Her final sacrifice on Vormir ensured the Soul Stone could be claimed, enabling the Time Heist and the restoration of the universe.
IN MEMORY: THE SPY WHO CHOSE HER FAMILY
VISION
VICTOR SHADE — 2015–2023
Born from the Mind Stone and Ultron's ambition, Vision chose to become something more than his creators intended. His synthetic mind held infinite empathy, and his sacrifice at Thanos's hand proved that even an artificial being could understand the meaning of love.
IN MEMORY: THE BEING WHO LEARNED TO FEEL
